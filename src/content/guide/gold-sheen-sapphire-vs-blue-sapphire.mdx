They share a mineral name, but a fine blue sapphire and a Gold Sheen Sapphire are
judged by almost opposite standards. This article sits under [Gold Sheen Sapphire vs
other sapphires](/guide/gold-sheen-sapphire-vs-other-sapphires/).

<KeyTakeaways>
- Same species (**corundum**), completely different value criteria.
- **Blue sapphire:** prized for **transparent, even colour**; commonly **heat-treated**; faceted.
- **Gold Sheen Sapphire:** prized for a **moving golden sheen** from dense inclusions; **unheated**; cut as a cabochon.
- Judge blue sapphire like a **window**; judge Gold Sheen like a **mirror**.
</KeyTakeaways>

## Window vs mirror

A blue sapphire is graded like a **window**: the clearer and more evenly, richly
coloured, the better — inclusions are flaws. A Gold Sheen Sapphire is graded like a
**mirror**: the quality of the surface reflection (the sheen) is everything, and the
inclusions are the feature that creates it. See [what Gold Sheen Sapphire
is](/guide/what-is-gold-sheen-sapphire/) for why.

## Side by side

| | Gold Sheen Sapphire | Blue sapphire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valued for | Golden sheen (schiller) | Transparent blue colour |
| Cut | Cabochon | Usually faceted |
| Treatment | Characteristically unheated | Frequently heat-treated |
| Clarity role | Dense inclusions = the feature | Fewer inclusions = better |
| Source | Single depleted Kenyan deposit | Many countries |

## Treatment: a key distinction

Most blue sapphire on the market is **heated** to improve colour and clarity — a
long-accepted practice. Gold Sheen Sapphire is valued **unheated**, because heat would
harm the very inclusions that create the sheen. If you care about natural, untreated
stones, that's a meaningful difference — and one to confirm with a report, as covered
in the [buying guide](/guide/gold-sheen-sapphire-buying-guide/).

## Value

Neither is simply "worth more." A fine, untreated blue sapphire of good origin can be
extremely valuable; Gold Sheen Sapphire's value rests on **sheen quality plus finite
supply** rather than transparent colour. They serve different tastes.
