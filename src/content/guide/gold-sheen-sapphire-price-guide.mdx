"How much is it worth?" is the most common question about Gold Sheen Sapphire — and
the honest answer is that there's no fixed price sheet. What there *is* is a clear
set of factors that move value up or down, and a fair way to compare stones. This
article expands the pricing section of the [buying
guide](/guide/gold-sheen-sapphire-buying-guide/).

<KeyTakeaways>
- There is **no single per-carat price** for a phenomenal, single-source gem.
- **Sheen quality** is the biggest lever, followed by colour, cut, size and scarcity.
- Compare **like with like**, under the **same light**, ranking by sheen first.
- Require **independent certification** for higher-value stones.
- Treat "investment" framing with caution — this is **informational, not financial advice**.
</KeyTakeaways>

## Why there's no single "per-carat" price

Transparent gems can be roughly indexed by colour and clarity grades. A phenomenal,
single-source stone can't be reduced that way: two stones of identical weight can
differ greatly in the quality of their sheen, and therefore in value. Anyone quoting
one flat number is oversimplifying.

## The factors that drive price

1. **Sheen quality — the biggest lever.** Strength, evenness and coverage of the
   golden band. A vivid, full, well-travelled sheen is worth a clear premium.
2. **Body colour.** Not "better or worse," but how well the colour frames and
   contrasts the sheen.
3. **Cut and orientation.** Symmetry, dome height, polish, and — critically —
   whether the sheen is correctly oriented to the face.
4. **Size.** Larger stones are rarer and cost more per carat, but never outrank a
   better sheen.
5. **Scarcity.** The deposit is depleted, so supply is fixed — a structural support
   under the whole category.

## A fair way to compare stones

- Compare **like with like**: similar size, similar cut, judged under the **same
  light**.
- Rank by **sheen first**, then colour, then cut, then size.
- Discount for problems: patchy sheen, poor orientation, dull polish, damage.
- For anything significant, require **independent certification** (natural, unheated)
  — see [how to spot a fake](/guide/how-to-spot-fake-gold-sheen-sapphire/).

## A note on "investment"

Because the supply is finite, Gold Sheen Sapphire is sometimes framed as an
investment. Treat that carefully.
