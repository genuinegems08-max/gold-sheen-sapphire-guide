The sheen that makes Gold Sheen Sapphire special is a *surface* effect, so a clean
surface matters more than for most gems. The good news is that safe cleaning takes
five minutes and nothing exotic. This is a quick companion to the [care &
maintenance pillar](/guide/gold-sheen-sapphire-care-and-maintenance/).

<KeyTakeaways>
- Use **warm water, mild dish soap and a soft brush** — nothing else.
- **Never** use ultrasonic or steam cleaners on this stone.
- Rinse over a bowl, dry with a **soft, lint-free cloth**.
- Put jewellery on **after** lotions and perfume to keep the sheen bright.
- Clean a daily-worn ring **every week or two**, or whenever it looks dull.
</KeyTakeaways>

## What you need

- A small bowl of **warm** (not hot) water
- A drop of **mild dish soap**
- A **soft brush** (a soft toothbrush is perfect)
- A **soft, lint-free cloth**

That's it — no chemicals, no machines.

## The step-by-step

1. **Soak** the piece in the warm soapy water for a few minutes to loosen oils and
   residue.
2. **Brush** gently around the stone and setting, paying attention to the underside
   of the gem where grime collects.
3. **Rinse** in clean water — over a bowl, never an open drain.
4. **Dry and buff** with the soft cloth to bring back the sheen.

## What to avoid

- **Ultrasonic cleaners** and **steam cleaners** — vibration and heat are an
  unnecessary risk for a stone full of inclusions.
- **Hot water and sudden temperature changes.**
- **Bleach, chlorine and harsh solvents.**
- **Abrasive cloths or pastes** that can dull the polish.

<Callout type="warning" title="Why not ultrasonic?">
Gold Sheen Sapphire's beauty comes from a dense inclusion field. High-energy
cleaning that's fine for a clean, faceted stone is best avoided here — warm soapy
water does the job safely.
</Callout>

## Keeping it clean for longer

Put jewellery on **after** lotions and perfume, take rings off for rough work, and
store each piece separately. Small habits keep the sheen bright between cleans. For
the full routine — wear, storage and check-ups — see the [care & maintenance
pillar](/guide/gold-sheen-sapphire-care-and-maintenance/), and for protective
settings, [Jewellery & settings](/guide/gold-sheen-sapphire-jewelry-and-settings/).
